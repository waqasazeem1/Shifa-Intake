The Healthcare Problem

The Critical Communication & Safety Gap

Millions of clinic consultations in Pakistan and South Asia face 4 major bottlenecks:

1. The Language Barrier

Patients express symptoms in Roman Urdu, Urdu script, or regional dialects, but medical education and clinic EHRs are strictly in English.

2. Dangerous Medication Errors

Over 60% of patients cannot understand complex English dosage instructions on discharge papers, leading to incorrect timing, missed doses, and relapses.

3. Overwhelmed Reception & Nursing Staff

Nurses in busy OPDs spend 10–15 minutes per patient translating free-text symptoms onto paper forms, creating massive patient queues.

4. Unnoticed Emergencies in Waiting Rooms

Cardiac symptoms, stroke signs, or breathing distress can sit waiting for hours without immediate rule-based prioritization.

Our Solution

Shifa Intake: Safe Bilingual Clinical Assistant

An end-to-end clinical workflow connecting patients, clinic staff, and doctors:

1. Speak or Type in Urdu

Patients speak via microphone (live Urdu speech recognition) or type everyday Roman Urdu text (e.g. "mery nose m drd hai 7 din sy").

2. Instant AI Extraction (<0.25s)

Extracts Chief Complaint, Duration, Symptoms, Medications, Allergies, and History with uncertainty badges for missing fields.

3. Rule-Based Red Flag Alerts

Transparent, hard-coded safety rules immediately detect chest pain, stroke, or breathing trouble independent of the LLM.

4. Staff Review & Corrections

Staff verify and correct fields on-screen before the case reaches the doctor. Full Human-in-the-Loop oversight.

5. 3-Step Doctor Advice

Doctor instructions are structured into 3 clear steps: 1) Medicine & Dosage, 2) Home Care & Diet, 3) Warning Signs.

6. Roman Urdu Audio Playback

Patients receive clean step-cards in Roman Urdu and Urdu script, accompanied by spoken audio they can listen to directly.

Safety Architecture

Guardrails-First: Zero Hallucination Risk

Why healthcare AI must enforce rigorous boundaries rather than free-form chatbots:

🚨 Independent Rule-Based Red Flags

Cardiac, respiratory, stroke, and self-harm keywords are caught by transparent regex patterns—never left to probabilistic LLM judgment.

⚠️ Uncertainty Tracking (No Guessing)

If a patient does not state an allergy or medical history, the schema tags it as "Uncertain / Not stated" rather than hallucinating details.

🚫 Anti-Diagnosis Regex Guardrail

Automated output filter blocks any generated text resembling automated diagnoses (e.g. "you have...", "prescribed...").

🔒 Enforced Human-in-the-Loop State Machine

The API enforces strict order: Cannot translate before intake approval, cannot release before doctor review. Zero autonomy.

Clinical Pipeline

End-to-End Clinical Flowchart

Strict state-machine order enforced by the FastAPI backend:

[Step 1: Patient Input]
 ↳ Urdu / Roman Urdu Voice or Text Input ➔ Rule-Based Red Flags Check (Cardiac / Stroke / Trauma)
[Step 2: AI Triage & Extraction (AMD ROCm)]
 ↳ Structured JSON: Complaint, Duration, Symptoms, Meds, Allergies, Uncertainty Badges
[Step 3: Human Staff Review Checkpoint] 🛡️
 ↳ Staff reviews fields, edits any unclear details, and clicks "Approve Intake"
[Step 4: Doctor Step-by-Step Instructions]
 ↳ Doctor provides 3 clear steps: 1) Medicine & Dose, 2) Home Care & Diet, 3) Warning Signs
[Step 5: Bilingual Translation & Native Audio Voice]
 ↳ Roman Urdu Step Cards + Urdu Script (اردو) + Spoken MP3 Audio Player ➔ Released to Patient
